Our real kit

The virtual lab is built from this photo of our department kit. The rule, clamps, masses and stand on screen are cut from it.

  • Centre-zero metre rule: 0 is in the middle, so the scale reading is the distance from the pivot.
  • Blue stand: the clamp at 0 sits in the V-notch at the top of the stand. This is the pivot.
  • Clamps with hooks: slide along the rule. Read the position at the slit in the centre of the clamp. Each clamp and hook has a mass of 5 g.
  • Hooked masses: 100 g has a weight of 1 N; 200 g has a weight of 2 N. A mass can hang from the ring under another mass.

How to use the virtual lab

  • Select a clamp: click it. A yellow outline shows which clamp you are using.
  • Move a clamp: drag it along the rule, or use the Move buttons (0.1 cm = 1 mm). Arrow keys also work.
  • Read a distance: use the close-up views. Read the scale at the red dashed line (the centre of the clamp) to the nearest 0.1 cm.
  • Hang masses: drag a mass from the bench onto a hook, or use Hang 100 g / Hang 200 g. Up to three masses can hang from one clamp.
  • Take a mass off: drag the bottom mass back to the bench, or use Take one off.
  • Is it level? If one side is heavier in turning effect, the rule tips until it rests on its stop or a mass touches the bench. When the moments balance, the rule stays level.
  • Extra clamps: drag a spare clamp from the bench onto the rule to try more than one force on a side.
